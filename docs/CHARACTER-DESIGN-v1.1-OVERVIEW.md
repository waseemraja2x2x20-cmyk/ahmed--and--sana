## CHARACTER DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

### Complementary Contrast (Visual & Personality)

**Physical Silhouette Contrast:**
- Ahmed: Slouched relaxed (173 cm) vs. Sana: Upright athletic (168 cm)
- Ahmed: Tousled dark curls vs. Sana: Layered chestnut with fringe
- Ahmed: Dark casual hoodie vs. Sana: Dusty rose knit + ivory trousers
- Ahmed: Hands-in-pockets default vs. Sana: Hand-on-hip confident stance

**Color Palette Harmony:**
- Ahmed: Cool navy jeans + dark charcoal vs. Sana: Warm dusty rose + ivory
- Ahmed: Brown leather watch accent vs. Sana: Tan crossbody + blue notebook
- Shared: Warm peachy-tan fair complexion = cohesive cast aesthetics
- Together: Cool + Warm balance = production-ready color design

**Expression & Personality Contrast:**
- Ahmed: Smug Smirk (mischievous) vs. Sana: Confident Smirk (witty challenge)
- Ahmed: Deep Overthinking Chin-Rest vs. Sana: Theatrical Eye-Roll (opposite minds)
- Ahmed: Soft Sincere Good-Heart Smile vs. Sana: Warm Genuine Laugh (authentic connection)
- Ahmed: Lazy shortcut thinking vs. Sana: Organized systematic approach

**Character Arc Parallelism:**
- Both start as individual competitors
- Both learn complementary strengths = greater outcomes
- Both reveal authentic warmth in climactic rooftop scene
- Both demonstrate mutual respect = series foundation for romance
