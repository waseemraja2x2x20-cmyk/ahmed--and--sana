### 4 Core Facial Acting Busts

#### 1. **Confident Competitive Smirk** (Assured, witty, ready to win)
- Arched right eyebrow raised slightly higher (mirror-opposite to Ahmed's overthinking tell)
- Raised corner of mouth, knowing satisfied expression
- Eyes sharp and direct, competitive gleam
- **Animation Use:** SC03 (challenge acceptance — "Finally"), SC04 (race confidence), SC06 (scoreboard updates), SC11 (rematch challenge acceptance)

#### 2. **Theatrical Exaggerated Eye-Roll** (Dismissive humor, comedic exasperation)
- Eyes rolled upward dramatically with visible white
- Eyebrows raised high in mock offense or comedic disbelief
- Mouth slightly open or pursed in theatrical reaction
- **Animation Use:** SC02 (reaction to Ahmed), SC03 ("You call that a system?"), SC05 (dismissal moment), SC07 (partnership resistance)

#### 3. **Dawning Epiphany Realization** (Moment of understanding, character growth)
- Eyes wide open with clarity, pupils focused inward-then-outward realization
- Eyebrows lifted in surprise-turning-to-understanding
- Mouth slightly open in "oh!" moment of intellectual breakthrough
- **Animation Use:** SC09 (understanding Ahmed's shortcut value), SC10 (realizing partnership strength), rematch engine setup
- **Story Significance:** Visual representation of Sana's emotional intelligence overcoming initial rigid thinking

#### 4. **Warm Genuine Laugh** (Authentic joy, emotional openness)
- Full genuine smile reaching eyes (Duchenne smile, eye crinkles)
- Eyebrows softly relaxed in natural arched position
- Eyes warm, crescent-shaped from genuine happiness
- Head tilt slightly back in natural laugh posture
- **Animation Use:** SC11-10 rooftop silence beat (shares frame with Ahmed's Good Heart Smile), post-rematch celebration
- **Story Significance:** Mirrors Ahmed's Good Heart Smile — mutual authentic connection. Validates Sana's "Caring" + "Emotionally Smart" traits. Proves partnership is genuine mutual respect, not just competitive respect.

## DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS FOR ANIMATION

### 2D Cel Animation Lineart
- **Line Weight:** Consistent 2-3px crisp strokes with slightly sharper angles than Ahmed (reflects confident personality)
- **Fill Strategy:** Flat color fills with 1-2 shadow layers, strategic gradients in hair for layered texture
- **Expressions:** Responsive eyebrow controls for theatrical range, mouth shape variety for comedic timing
- **Silhouette:** Distinctive long hair + fringe creates unique readable silhouette at thumbnail scale

### Rigging Requirements (Phase 2)
- **Head Rig:** Jaw rotation, eyebrow controls (L/R independent with enhanced range), eye direction, fringe sway
- **Body Rig:** Torso bend, shoulder rotation, arm IK/FK, hip movement (athletic posture)
- **Prop Handling:** Hands rigged for Notebook holding (essential prop), other prop poses as needed
- **Hair Rig:** Layered hair strands with subtle sway physics, fringe responsive to head turns
- **Viseme Targets:** Ee, W-Oo, Oh, F (lip-sync animation ready, extended range for comedic dialogue)

### Animation Cycles (Phase 2)
- **Walk Cycle:** Confident athletic gait, purposeful stride with natural shoulder movement
- **Run Cycle:** Competitive race dynamic (SC04), athletic speed, natural competitive drive
- **Gesture Library:** Point (accusatory), Fist (emphasis), Eye-roll (comedic dismissal), Hand-to-hip (confident stance), Notebook adjustment (prop-based gesture)
- **Lip-Sync Masters:** All 21 Sana dialogue lines with extended comedic timing, rhythmic delivery emphasis

## SANA & AHMED PARTNERSHIP VISUAL DESIGN

### Complementary Character Design Philosophy

**Visual Contrast (Drama):**
- Ahmed: Slouched relaxed posture vs. Sana: Upright confident stance
- Ahmed: Messy tousled curls vs. Sana: Layered controlled long hair
- Ahmed: Dark hoodie casual vs. Sana: Dusty rose knit + ivory trousers styled
- Ahmed: Asymmetrical overthinking eyebrow vs. Sana: Arched competitive eyebrows

**Color Contrast (Visual Balance):**
- Ahmed: Dark charcoal hoodie + indigo jeans vs. Sana: Dusty rose + ivory
- Ahmed: Warm brown eyes + brown leather watch vs. Sana: Hazel-brown eyes + tan crossbody
- Together: Cool (Ahmed's navy) + Warm (Sana's rose) = balanced aesthetic
- Shared warm peachy-tan fair complexions = cohesive color palette unity

**Expression Contrast (Dynamic Chemistry):**
- Ahmed: Smug Smirk (playful mischief) vs. Sana: Confident Smirk (witty challenge)
- Ahmed: Deep Overthinking Chin-Rest vs. Sana: Theatrical Eye-Roll (opposite cognitive styles)
- Ahmed: Soft Sincere Good-Heart Smile vs. Sana: Warm Genuine Laugh (authentic mutual connection)

**Character Arc Parallelism:**
- Both start competitive, individual-focused
- Both learn complementary approaches strengthen outcomes
- Both reveal authentic warmth in rooftop silence beat
- Series foundation: mutual respect + genuine partnership = rom-com gold
