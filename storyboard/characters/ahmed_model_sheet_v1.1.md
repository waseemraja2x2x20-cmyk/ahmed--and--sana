### 4 Core Facial Acting Busts

#### 1. **Smug Smirk** (Confident, playful banter)
- Raised right eyebrow (asymmetrical overthinking tell)
- Slight lift at corner of mouth, knowing expression
- Eyes half-lidded with charm, direct gaze
- **Animation Use:** SC03 (challenge acceptance), SC04 (race confidence), SC06 (false success moment)

#### 2. **Deep Overthinking with Chin Rest** (Introspection, calculation)
- Right hand supporting chin (signature pose)
- Both eyebrows furrowed, focused inward gaze
- Lips slightly parted (mid-thought), intense concentration
- **Animation Use:** SC05 (puzzle contemplation), SC07 (failure realization), SC09 (solution dawning)

#### 3. **Wide-Eyed Calculation Panic** (Moment of crisis)
- Eyes wide open with visible white, eyebrows raised high
- Mouth slightly open in surprise or concern
- Direct forward gaze, alert posture
- **Animation Use:** SC04 (hedge shortcut failure), SC08 (box mechanism jam), SC10 (rematch challenge)

#### 4. **Soft Sincere Good-Heart Smile** (Genuine warmth, emotional depth) ⭐ CRITICAL
- Genuine smile reaching eyes (Duchenne smile, eye crinkles)
- Eyebrows softly relaxed, natural arched position
- Eyes warm and open, looking slightly down (introspective kindness)
- **Animation Use:** SC11-10 rooftop silence beat (CRITICAL for authentic Good Heart reveal) — 2.0 second hold, no dialogue deflection
- **Story Significance:** Validates Ahmed's character arc: lazy exterior conceals genuine emotional capacity. This smile is the earned payoff for partnership authenticity.

## DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS FOR ANIMATION

### 2D Cel Animation Lineart
- **Line Weight:** Consistent 2-3px crisp strokes
- **Fill Strategy:** Flat color fills with 1-2 shadow layers
- **Expressions:** Minimal line modification (eyebrows, mouth) for efficient cel rigging
- **Silhouette:** Readable at thumbnail scale for storyboard legibility

### Rigging Requirements (Phase 2)
- **Head Rig:** Jaw rotation, eyebrow controls (L/R independent), eye direction
- **Body Rig:** Torso bend, shoulder rotation, arm IK/FK
- **Prop Handling:** Hands rigged for Book, Screwdriver, Blueprint, Trophy holding poses
- **Viseme Targets:** Ee, W-Oo, Oh, F (lip-sync animation ready)

### Animation Cycles (Phase 2)
- **Walk Cycle:** Relaxed slouched gait, casual stride
- **Run Cycle:** Efficient shortcut-seeking movement, playful urgency
- **Gesture Library:** Point (accusatory), Fist (emphasis), Eye-roll (dismissal), Shrug (lazy deflection)
- **Lip-Sync Masters:** All 45 dialogue lines with viseme consistency
