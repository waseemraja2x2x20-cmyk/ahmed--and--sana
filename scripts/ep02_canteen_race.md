## Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: The Last Samosa (00:00 to 00:20)
- **00:00** Canteen, lunch rush. SFX: crowd murmur + clattering trays.
- **00:03** Ahmed at one table, Sana at another. Both eating, ignoring each other.
- **00:06** **CANTEEN WORKER** (offscreen, calling): "Last samosa! Ek hi bacha hai!"
- **00:08** Both heads snap up at the same time. **TRIGGER:** Key Q (white flash freeze frame). SFX: record scratch.
- **00:10** Freeze frame holds: both glaring at each other across the canteen. SFX: spaghetti western whistle. A lone roti rolls past like a tumbleweed.
- **00:14** **AHMED** (low, firm): "Mine."
- **00:15** **SANA** (low, firm): "Mine."
- **00:16** Both said it simultaneously. Beat of silence. 4 frames hold.
- **00:18** Both slam trays down and rise at the same time. SFX: tray clatter.

### Scene 2: The Race (00:20 to 00:50)
- **00:20** **TRIGGER:** Key W ON (both running poses) + Key 9 ON (speed lines + dust). SFX: race buzzer. Fast footsteps loop starts.
- **00:22** They sprint screen-left to screen-right, toward the counter. **NOTE:** this episode they run the SAME direction, no face-off rules here.
- **00:24** Sana overtakes on the outside. Ahmed cuts inside. Elbow nudge.
- **00:28** **SANA** (mid-run, shouting): "Move it, hoodie!"
- **00:30** **AHMED** (mid-run): "You already had lunch!"
- **00:31** **TRIGGER:** Key 2 ARM (Sana pulls magazine out mid-run, holds it like a relay baton). SFX: whip swish.
- **00:32** Ahmed flinches sideways, loses half a step. SFX: gulp.
- **00:34** Obstacle: a friend crosses carrying a tall stack of plates. Both split around him, plates wobble comically. SFX: ceramic wobble rattle.
- **00:38** Ahmed clips a chair leg, stumbles. **TRIGGER:** Key 8 ON (Ahmed worried face). SFX: stumble thud.
- **00:40** Sana gains a full lead. Dust trail behind her thickens (Key 9 stays on).
- **00:42** **SANA** (glancing back, Key 1 ON: point + smirk): "Too slow, genius!"
- **00:45** **TRIGGER:** Key E (Ahmed desperate dive lunge, one-shot, 24 frames). SFX: heroic whoosh + gasp from background students.
- **00:48** Both hands reach for the counter at the same instant...
- **00:50** COLLISION. Both grab the samosa simultaneously and it launches upward. **TRIGGER:** Key T (samosa projectile visible) + Key 0 (impact FX). SFX: crash + everything drops into slow motion filter.

### Scene 3: The Slow Motion Samosa (00:50 to 01:10)
- **00:53** **TRIGGER:** Key E + Key R (both tumble poses frozen mid-air). SFX: slow-mo heartbeat.
- **00:55** Slow motion: the samosa rotates in the air above them. Key the arc across 72 frames (1320 to 1391). Both stare up, mouths `mouth_O`.
- **00:58** **TRIGGER:** Key Y ON (shared imagination bubble above both heads). Inside bubble: the samosa landing safely on a plate, both eating happily, sitting together on the same bench. (Draw this bubble art.)
- **01:02** Bubble pops on the next cue (Key Y OFF, Key T OFF). Reality: the samosa descends...
- **01:05** ...and lands with a perfect PLOP on the **PROFESSOR'S** plate as he sits down at the canteen table with his lunch tray. SFX: soft plop.
- **01:06** Total silence. SFX: single cricket chirp.
- **01:07** Professor looks down at the plate. Looks up over his glasses. Slow zoom on his face. 12 frames.
- **01:08** **PROFESSOR** (calm, which is worse): "Ahmed. Sana. My office. NOW." **TRIGGER:** Key 4 ON (Ahmed shocked) + Key 6 ON (Sana shocked). SFX: sting.
- **01:09** Both frozen mid-tumble, staring. 4 frames hold. Cut.

### Scene 4: Detention, Geometry Edition (01:10 to 01:40)
- **01:10** Detention room. Both sitting at ONE desk, back to back, arms crossed. Reuse the Episode 1 sulking pose (Key 5 for Ahmed, mirrored for Sana).
- **01:14** **AHMED** (grumbling): "This is your fault."
- **01:16** **SANA** (grumbling): "You leapt like a scared cat."
- **01:17** **TRIGGER:** Key 7 ON (Sana angry brows) briefly, then OFF.
- **01:18** Beat. Both glance at each other. Look away. 8 frames of silence.
- **01:20** **PROFESSOR** (entering, places ONE samosa on a plate between them): "Since you both love it so much. Share. Equally. I will be watching."
- **01:23** Professor sits at his desk in the background, watching. SFX: chair drag.
- **01:24** Slow zoom on the single samosa between them. SFX: suspense zoom whoosh.
- **01:26** Sana slowly pulls out a ruler. Ahmed pulls out his geometry compass set.
- **01:28** **SANA** (dead serious): "Forty-five degree cut. Not one crumb from my side."
- **01:30** **AHMED** (dead serious): "Perpendicular bisector. I already measured."
- **01:33** Both cut at the exact same time. SFX: two ruler chops + crunch. **TRIGGER:** Key 0 (tiny impact FX on the cut, comic touch).
- **01:34** Both chew. Both look at each other. Both chewing. 8 frames.
- **01:35** **AHMED** (small smirk): "...Rematch at the chai stall?"
- **01:37** **SANA** (small smirk): "Obviously. Loser pays."
- **01:38** FREEZE FRAME: back to back, each holding half a samosa like a trophy. End card slam: "AHMED vs SANA" + big VS. SFX: drum hit. Cut to black.
- **01:40** End.

## Animation Notes
- Facing rule exception: in the race they BOTH run screen-left to screen-right. Keep Ahmed slightly behind Sana until 00:45 so the dive reads as a real chance.
- Run cycle: build `leg_run_1` to `leg_run_4` as a swap set and cycle it every 4 frames. Sana's back-hair rig layer (`hair_back`) from the existing split-hair setup should lag 2 frames behind the head.
- Slow motion: keep rendering at 24 fps, just stretch the samosa arc keyframes across 72 frames, ending on frame 1391 so the bubble can pop on at frame 1392. Do not change project frame rate.
- The imagination bubble art (both friends eating together happily) is the hidden heart of the episode. Keep it on screen from the `00:58` cue until the `01:02` pop cue.
- Professor calm voice is scarier than shouting. Record it flat and slow.
- Ahmed flinch at 00:32 must be small (3-4 frames), it costs him the race and sets up "You leapt like a scared cat" in Scene 4.
- Freeze frame holds from the 00:10 stare beat until Ahmed's 00:14 line, with the whistle carrying the moment, and the 01:38 ending freeze should carry straight through the end-card beat until the cut at 01:40.

5. Record trigger pass: race first (keys W, 9, E, R, T), then reaction pass (keys 1, 4, 6, 7, 8, Q, Y).
6. Arm puppets one at a time for the W running trigger pass, since Character Animator records the shared W trigger separately per puppet, then sync both recorded W passes to the shared `00:20` start in the timeline.
7. Clean timing against the table above.
8. Export 1080p, 24 fps to `exports/final/` as `ep02_canteen_race.mp4`.
